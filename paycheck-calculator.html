Hourly or salary

The calculator supports hourly workers and salaried employees. Hourly users can enter regular hours and overtime hours to see how extra shifts may change take-home pay.

All-state baseline

The state selector includes all 50 states plus Washington DC. West Virginia uses official withholding, no-wage-tax states show $0 state wage tax, and other states use baseline 2026 income-tax estimates.

Private by design

Your numbers are calculated in your browser. Saved settings stay on your device, and the calculator does not require an account.

Estimate notes

TakeHomeCalc estimates federal withholding, FICA payroll taxes, and state income tax where supported. Local taxes, employer-specific payroll methods, bonuses, and unusual deductions are not included.
