How the Florida estimate works

Florida has no state wage income tax in this baseline calculator, so the state wage tax line is estimated as $0.00. Federal withholding, Social Security, Medicare, and deductions still affect take-home pay.

Use it for raises

The raise calculator estimates how much more you may take home per paycheck, per month, and per year.

What is not included

Local taxes, special payroll rules, bonuses, commissions, and employer-specific deductions are not modeled. Actual paychecks may vary.
