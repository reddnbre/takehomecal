About TakeHomeCalc

What TakeHomeCalc Does

TakeHomeCalc is a free browser-based paycheck calculator for estimating regular pay, overtime pay, and raise scenarios. It is built for U.S. workers who want a clearer view of take-home pay before making work and budget decisions.

How Estimates Work

The calculator estimates federal withholding, Social Security, Medicare, common pre-tax deductions, post-tax deductions, and state wage income tax. West Virginia uses an official withholding path. Other taxable states use baseline 2026 state income-tax estimates.

Privacy First

Calculations run in your browser. The site does not require an account, and your paycheck inputs are not sent to a TakeHomeCalc server.

Limits

TakeHomeCalc is an estimate only. Employer payroll methods, local taxes, special deductions, bonuses, year-to-date wages, and personal tax circumstances can change actual paycheck results.

Important Disclaimer

TakeHomeCalc provides information for general planning purposes only. It is not tax, legal, payroll, or financial advice. For decisions that affect taxes, benefits, or legal obligations, consider speaking with a qualified professional.
