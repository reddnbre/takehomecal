Paycheck Withholding Resources

How Paycheck Withholding Works

Payroll systems estimate income tax withholding from taxable wages, pay frequency, filing status, W-4 adjustments, and tax tables. Social Security and Medicare are calculated separately.

How Overtime Pay Works

Most overtime is paid at time-and-a-half. This calculator compares the full paycheck before and after overtime to estimate the incremental take-home amount.

Is Overtime Taxed More?

Overtime is not subject to a special higher income tax rate just because it is overtime. A larger paycheck may have larger withholding.

Withholding vs Actual Tax Liability

Your paycheck withholding and your final income tax liability can differ. Your tax return reconciles those amounts using your annual income and deductions.

How 401(k) Contributions Affect Take-Home Pay

Traditional 401(k) contributions usually reduce federal and state taxable wages, but they do not automatically reduce every payroll tax. Deduction treatment can vary.

How To Calculate Time-and-a-Half

Multiply the regular hourly rate by 1.5, then multiply by overtime hours. The take-home effect is lower after withholding and deductions.
