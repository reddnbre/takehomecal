Privacy Policy

No Account Required

TakeHomeCalc does not require users to create an account, sign in, or submit personal payroll information to use the calculator.

In-Browser Calculations

Paycheck, overtime, and raise calculations run in your browser. Your pay inputs are used on your device to calculate estimates and are not sent to a TakeHomeCalc server.

Saved Settings

If you choose to remember pay settings, those settings may be saved in your browser storage on your device. You can clear saved settings from the calculator or through your browser.

No Selling Personal Data

TakeHomeCalc does not sell personal payroll data. The site is designed as a simple static tool without user accounts or a user database.

Third-Party Links

The site may link to GitHub, Buy Me a Coffee, tax sources, affiliate partners, or other third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Advertising Partners

TakeHomeCalc may use Google AdSense or other advertising partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, and help prevent fraud.

Google Ads and Cookies

Google may use information from sites or apps that use its services to deliver and measure ads. You can learn more at How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

Users can manage personalized advertising settings through their Google account and browser settings. Browser controls may also let you block or delete cookies.

Advice Disclaimer

TakeHomeCalc provides paycheck estimates for informational purposes only. It is not tax, legal, payroll, or financial advice.

Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated as the site changes. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version of this policy.
